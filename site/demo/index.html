One uncut run.

The maze hunt as the ghosts: possession switches, three catches to find, and the Menace taking his power pellets — each one tearing the maze open into a different game. A kart Grand Prix with a held drift, a first-person corridor hunt, a block kingdom, a ring valley, then back to the maze. No cuts, no staging: the shipped build, played start to finish.

This capture is silent and AI-driven, recorded on the shipped build; the game itself plays with its own original WebAudio score. Prefer to drive the ghosts yourself? Play The Round Menace in your browser.
