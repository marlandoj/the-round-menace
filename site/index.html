You know this maze. The pellets, the corners, the chase. This time, you're the ghosts — and he's eating your maze.

Free in your browser. No install, no account — keyboard or touch.

Arrows or WASD steer. 1–4 possess a ghost. Space hops, pauses — and drifts.
The flips — one uncut run

Every power pellet is a different game.

One continuous run: the maze hunt, then the Menace takes his pellets and the maze tears open — a kart Grand Prix with a held drift, a first-person corridor hunt, a block kingdom, a ring valley. All four flips in a single take, and back to the maze to finish the hunt.

A silent capture of an AI-driven run on the shipped build — the game itself plays with its own original WebAudio score, so keep the sound on in the player above. Prefer to watch it with narration? The demo page is here.

How to play

Hunt. Possess. Survive the flip.

  1. Catch him three times. Steer your ghost with the arrows, WASD or a swipe. The Menace is clearing the maze pellet by pellet — every pellet he eats is ground you lose. Three catches end his run; an emptied maze ends yours.
  2. You are four ghosts. Ember chases, Rosé ambushes, Tide flanks and Goldie keeps her distance. Press 1–4 or tap a ghost to possess it — the rest hunt on their own instincts. You don't steer a ghost; you coordinate a squad.
  3. When he takes a power pellet, the hunt flips. Each corner tears the maze into a different game, with you in his shoes: a valley run, a block kingdom, a corridor hunt, a grand prix. Win the flip and the pickups you grabbed pour back into the maze as pellets. Lose, and your ghost rides the bench.
